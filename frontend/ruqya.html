Advanced Ruqya Guide

Recitation of the Quran as healing and protection from spiritual afflictions

🕌 Stage 1: Self-Ruqya (الرقية على النفس)

الرقية الشرعية — الشرعية هي المعتبرة

You must perform Ruqya on yourself before anyone else. This is your first line of defense. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Show me your ruqya, for indeed the jinn do not object to the Quran."

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ (×3)
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ (×3)
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ (×3)
Start with Basmala, then recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas × 3 each

Daily Self-Ruqya Protocol:

  • 1
    Morning Adhkar: Recite Ayat al-Kursi × 1 after each of the 5 prayers. Recite Surah Al-Baqarah (286 verses) — if too long, recite the last 2 verses. Recite Al-Mu'awwidhatayn × 3.
  • 2
    Evening Adhkar: Recite Ayat al-Kursi × 1 after Maghrib. Recite Surah Al-Mulk (67). Recite Surah As-Sajdah (32). Recite Al-Mu'awwidhatayn × 3.
  • 3
    Before Sleep: Recite Ayat al-Kursi × 1. Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas × 3, Al-Falaq × 3, An-Nas × 3. Blow into hands and wipe over body × 3. Recite the last 2 verses of Surah Al-Baqarah.
  • 4
    Upon Waking: Recite "Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim" × 3. Recite "La ilaha illa Anta, Subhanaka, inni kuntu min ad-dhalimin" × 3 (Surah Al-Anbiya 21:87 — Yunus).

🤲 Stage 2: Ruqya on Others (الرقية على غيره)

إذا رقى فليقل: بسم الله

When performing Ruqya on someone else, recite with your right hand placed on the affected area. Blow gently after each recitation.

Key Ruqya Sessions for Others:

  • 1
    Session Setup: Face Qibla if possible. Begin with Basmala. Place right hand on the person's forehead or affected area. Recite with conviction and authority.
  • 2
    Opening Verses: Ayat al-Kursi × 3. Surah Al-Baqarah:255-257. Surah Al-Baqarah:286 (last 2 verses).
  • 3
    Core Ruqya: Surah Al-Fatihah × 7. Surah Ya-Sin × 3. Surah Al-Anbiya (21:87) × 100 if necessary.
  • 4
    Protection Surahs: Surah Al-Mu'awwidhatayn × 3. Surah Al-Falaq × 3. Surah An-Nas × 3.
  • 5
    Closing: Blow gently into hands and wipe over the person. Repeat Basmala × 3. Make du'a for healing.

⚠️ Important Warnings

  • Never use names of jinn or shayateen — only use names of Allah
  • Never ask jinn to leave — command them in the name of Allah
  • Never perform Ruqya on someone without their knowledge or consent
  • The Ruqi (one who performs Ruqya) must be a practicing Muslim
  • Never use talismans (taweez) with Quran — only use water or oil
  • The person being treated must also increase their own worship

🔒 Stage 3: Captage — Binding Jinn (الكبت)

الكبت — أسر الجن بالرقية الشرعية

Captage is the process of binding jinn using Quranic verses. This is done when a jinn refuses to leave after standard Ruqya. This is NOT the first resort — it is the last resort.

✅ When to Use Captage

  • The jinn has been identified through symptoms and responds to Ruqya
  • Standard Ruqya has failed after multiple sessions (7+ sessions)
  • The jinn speaks through the person and refuses to leave
  • The practitioner is qualified and experienced

Captage Procedure:

  • 1
    Preparation: The person must be in a state of wudu. The practitioner must have strong iman and be free from major sins. The environment must be clean and free from haram items.
  • 2
    Identification: Ask the jinn (through the person): "Who are you? Why are you here? What do you want?" The jinn must answer in the name of Allah. If it refuses, recite stronger verses.
  • 3
    Negotiation: If the jinn speaks, negotiate. Ask what it wants. If it wants to leave, let it leave with Basmala. If it refuses, proceed to binding.
  • 4
    Binding (Al-Kabt): Recite Surah Al-Baqarah:255-257 × 3. Recite Ayat al-Kursi × 7. Recite Surah Al-Baqarah:286 × 7. Command: "In the name of Allah, I bind you and prevent you from harming this person."
  • 5
    Sealing: Recite Surah Al-Fatihah × 1. Blow into water or oil. The person drinks the water or applies the oil. The jinn is bound until it agrees to leave or until Allah wills.

⛔ Dangers of Captage

  • Only qualified practitioners should attempt captage
  • A weak practitioner may be attacked by the jinn
  • The jinn may become violent during binding
  • Never use force — use authority of Quran only
  • The person must maintain their salah and dhikr throughout

📚 Ben Halima Abderraouf — Key Teachings

Ben Halima's methodology emphasizes these principles:

  • 1
    Pure Quranic Ruqya Only: Use only Quranic verses and prophetic du'as. No sihr, no amulets, no names of jinn. The Quran is sufficient.
  • 2
    Stages of Treatment: (1) Self-Ruqya first, (2) Ruqya on others with consent, (3) Captage only when necessary. Never skip stages.
  • 3
    The Jinn Must Answer in Allah's Name: If a jinn speaks through the person, it must answer questions "Bi-ismillah" (In the name of Allah). If it refuses, it is a Shaytan.
  • 4
    Identify Before Treating: Determine if the affliction is jinn, evil eye, or waswas (whispering). Each has different treatment.
  • 5
    Patience and Persistence: Ruqya is not instant. It requires multiple sessions, sometimes months. The patient must maintain salah and dhikr.
  • 6
    Water and Oil Method: Recite Quran into water (for drinking) or oil (for applying). This is the sunnah method. Never use taweez (paper amulets).
  • 7
    The Strong Believer: A believer with strong iman is protected. Ruqya works best when the patient increases their ibadah (worship).
