# Self-Ruqya

> **Self-treatment** — Performing ruqya on oneself. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Morning-Evening-Protection]]

Self-ruqya involves reciting Quranic verses and authentic du'as over oneself for protection and healing. This is the preferred method as it strengthens one's connection with Allah.
