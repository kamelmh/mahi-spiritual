# Overdiagnosis

> **Ruqya concept** — Attributing everything to spiritual causes. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Medicine-and-Ruqya]]

Overdiagnosis in ruqya refers to attributing all problems to spiritual causes (jinn, evil eye, sihr) when they may have medical or psychological origins.
