# Tawheed in Ruqya

> **Core principle** — Oneness of Allah in ruqya practice. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Ruqya-vs-Shirk]]

Tawheed (monotheism) is the foundation of valid ruqya. The practitioner must believe that only Allah can cure; the Quran and du'as are merely means.
