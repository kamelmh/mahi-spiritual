## Daily Recitation Practice

### Morning (After Fajr)
1. **Al-Fatiha** (1x) — Opening prayer
2. **Day's Surah** (1x) — Complete recitation
3. **Yunus Dhikr** (100x) — "La ilaha illa Anta, Subhanaka, inni kuntu min al-dhalimeen"

### Afternoon (After Dhuhr)
1. **Ar-Rahman 55:1-4** (3x) — Teaching invocation
2. **Day's Surah** (1x) — Complete recitation

### Evening (After Maghrib)
1. **Al-Qalam 68:1** (3x) — Writing invocation
2. **Day's Surah** (1x) — Complete recitation
3. **Al-Ikhlas** (3x) — Sincerity closing

### Night (Before Sleep)
1. **Al-Mu'awwidhatayn** (An-Nas + Al-Falaq) — Protection
2. **Ayat al-Kursi** (1x) — Throne verse
3. **Day's Surah** (1x) — Complete recitation

## Tips for Success

1. **No Chopping:** Each Surah is recited COMPLETE — never split into parts
2. **Consistency:** Recite at the same time each day
3. **Understanding:** Read translation after Arabic recitation
4. **Journaling:** Write one insight after each recitation
5. **Flexibility:** If missed, continue next day — don't skip Surahs
6. **Community:** Share insights with family or study group
