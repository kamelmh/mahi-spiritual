# Evil Eye (Al-Ayn)

> **Spiritual concept** — Harm caused by envy or admiration. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Sihr-Magic]], [[Morning-Evening-Protection]]

Al-Ayn (the evil eye) is harm caused by looking with envy or admiration. Protection includes morning/evening adhkar, ruqya recitations, and specific supplications.
