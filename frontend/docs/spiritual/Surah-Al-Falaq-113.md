# Surah Al-Falaq (113)

> **Quranic chapter** — The Daybreak, seeking protection. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Surah-An-Naas-114]]

One of the Mu'awwidhatayn (two chapters of refuge). Seeks protection from the evil of creation, darkness, and envy. Recited daily for protection.
