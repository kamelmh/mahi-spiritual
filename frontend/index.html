Ruqya Guide

Islamic spiritual healing through Quranic recitation

What is Ruqya?

Ruqya (رقية) is the Islamic practice of spiritual healing through the recitation of Quranic verses and authentic supplications. It is a Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad (SAW) and a means of seeking Allah's help for physical, emotional, and spiritual ailments.

"And We send down of the Quran that which is a healing and a mercy to those who believe." — Surah Al-Isra 17:82

Key Principle: Healing comes from Allah alone. The Quran is the means, not the source. Never attribute healing to the words themselves — always to Allah.

Conditions for Valid Ruqya

📖
1. Quranic Verses Only
Use only Quranic verses or authentic supplications from the Sunnah. No foreign words or inventions.
🗣️
2. Arabic Language
Recite in Arabic or in a language the affected person understands. The words must be intelligible.
🤲
3. Belief in Allah Alone
Believe that healing comes from Allah alone. The ruqya practitioner is only a means, not the source.
🚫
4. No Shirk or Bid'ah
No polytheism (shirk), no innovation (bid'ah), no amulets, no reliance on jinn or created beings.

Self-Ruqya — Step-by-Step Guide

Self-ruqya is the preferred method as it strengthens your connection with Allah. Perform on yourself before seeking help from others.

1
Begin with Basmalah
Say "Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Raheem" 3 times. Place your right hand on the affected area.
2
Recite Ayat al-Kursi (2:255)
The Throne Verse — one of the most powerful verses for protection and healing. Recite 3 times.
3
Recite Surah Al-Fatiha (7 times)
The Opening — the greatest Surah. Blow gently on your palms and pass over the affected area.
4
Recite Al-Mu'awwidhatayn (3 times each)
Surah Al-Falaq (113) and Surah An-Nas (114) — the two refuges. Blow on palms and pass over body.
5
Make Dua to Allah
Ask Allah specifically for healing. Say: "Allahumma rabbi an-nas, adhhib al-ba'sa, wa ashfi anta ash-Shafi" (O Lord of people, remove the harm and heal, You are the Healer).

Distance Ruqya

Ruqya can be performed remotely for those who cannot perform it themselves.

Water Method
Recite Quranic verses over clean water. The affected person drinks the water or washes with it.
Oil Method
Recite over olive oil. The affected person applies it to their body, especially the forehead.
Dua Method
Make sincere dua for the person from a distance. Allah's mercy is not limited by space.

Emergency Verses

Quick-reference for urgent spiritual situations:

Anxiety / Worry

Ayat al-Kursi + Al-Mu'awwidhatayn before sleep
Morning / Evening Adhkar
Full morning and evening protection supplications daily

Section 1: Vedic Astrology Basics (Jyotish Shastra)

Vedic Astrology (Jyotish) is the ancient Indian science of light, dating back over 5,000 years to the Vedas. Unlike Western astrology which uses the tropical zodiac (seasonal), Jyotish uses the sidereal zodiac — fixed star positions — making it approximately 23°48' behind tropical (Lahiri ayanamsa). The word "Jyotish" means "light" — studying how celestial light shapes karma.

🪐 The 9 Planets (Grahas)

Grahas (meaning "to seize") are karmic forces that grip the soul. Each carries specific energies — they are not just celestial bodies but living agents of destiny. The two luminaries (Sun, Moon) plus 5 visible planets plus 2 mathematical nodes (Rahu, Ketu) form the Navagraha.

☉ Sun (Surya)
Soul, ego, authority, father, vitality. Rules Leo. Exalted in Aries (10°). Debilitated in Libra. Day star — represents the Atma (soul).
☽ Moon (Chandra)
Mind, emotions, mother, nurture. Rules Cancer. Exalted in Taurus. Debilitated in Scorpio. The Mind — represents how you think and feel.
♂ Mars (Mangala)
Courage, action, conflict, siblings. Rules Aries & Scorpio. Exalted in Capricorn. Debilitated in Cancer. The Commander — drives ambition and war.
☿ Mercury (Budha)
Intellect, communication, trade, education. Rules Gemini & Virgo. Exalted in Virgo. Debilitated in Pisces. The Merchant — governs speech and logic.
♃ Jupiter (Guru)
Wisdom, dharma, children, wealth. Rules Sagittarius & Pisces. Exalted in Cancer. Debilitated in Capricorn. The Teacher — represents expansion and grace.
♀ Venus (Shukra)
Love, luxury, art, marriage, comfort. Rules Taurus & Libra. Exalted in Pisces. Debilitated in Virgo. The Guru of Asuras — beauty and desire.
♄ Saturn (Shani)
Discipline, karma, delay, longevity. Rules Capricorn & Aquarius. Exalted in Libra. Debilitated in Aries. The Judge — rewards patience, punishes shortcuts.
☊ Rahu (North Node)
Obsession, illusion, foreign lands, sudden events. Not a physical planet — the lunar node where Moon crosses north. Rules Aquarius. Amplifies whatever it touches.
☋ Ketu (South Node)
Spirituality, detachment, past-life karma, liberation. The lunar node where Moon crosses south. Rules Scorpio. The Mokshakaraka — releases material bonds.

Section 5: Hellenistic Techniques (Ancient Western)

Hellenistic Astrology (150 BCE - 700 CE) is the Western tradition that shares roots with Vedic through Babylonian origins. While Vedic uses Nakshatras and Dasha systems, Hellenistic developed Whole Sign Houses, Planetary Sect, Lots (Arabic Parts), and Annual Profections — all used in MAHI's chart analysis alongside Jyotish.

Whole Sign Houses
The oldest house system: each sign = one house. The Ascendant sign becomes the 1st house, the next sign the 2nd, and so on. No intercepted signs, no unequal houses. Simple, powerful, ancient — and shared with Vedic astrology's Rashi chart.
In Your Chart: Gemini ASC → 1st house = all of Gemini, 2nd = all of Cancer, 3rd = all of Leo, etc. Sun/Mercury/Mars in Aquarius = 9th house. Saturn in Pisces = 10th house. Jupiter in Sagittarius = 7th house.
Planetary Sect (Day/Night Chart)
Each planet belongs to either the Day sect (Sun, Jupiter, Saturn) or Night sect (Moon, Venus, Mars). Planets work best when in their proper sect — diurnal planets during daytime, nocturnal at night. Sect determines ease or difficulty of expression.
In Your Chart: Born at 14:00 (daytime) = Day chart. Sun, Jupiter, Saturn are in sect (stronger). Moon, Venus, Mars are out of sect (more challenged). But Jupiter in domicile overrides sect — still powerful. Moon debilitated + out of sect = emotional challenges.
Lots (Arabic Parts / Planetary Lots)
Calculated points derived from three planetary positions (usually ASC + two planets). The Lot of Fortune = ASC + Moon - Sun (by day) or ASC + Sun - Moon (by night). The Lot of Spirit = inverse of Fortune. These lots reveal specific life domains — Fortune = body/circumstances, Spirit = soul/purpose.
In Your Chart: Fortune at Virgo 4°55' (4th house) — fortune through home, mother, property, inner peace. Spirit at Pisces 4°55' (10th house) — soul purpose through career, public image, karma. The fortune-body lives at home; the spirit-soul lives in the world.
Annual Profections (Age-Based Timing)
