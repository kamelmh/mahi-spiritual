## 12TH HARMONIC CHART (D12 — Dwadashamsha)

### What Is D12?

The D12 harmonic chart divides each sign into 12 equal parts of 2°30' each. It reveals:
- **Ancestral patterns** — inherited traits from parents/grandparents
- **Family karma** — what you carry from your lineage
- **Spiritual evolution** — soul-level development beyond the natal chart
- **Hidden talents** — abilities that don't show in the natal chart

### How to Calculate D12

For each planet at X° in sign Y:
1. Multiply the degree by 12
2. The remainder determines the D12 sign
3. Formula: `D12_sign = (floor(X * 12 / 30) + sign_index) mod 12`
