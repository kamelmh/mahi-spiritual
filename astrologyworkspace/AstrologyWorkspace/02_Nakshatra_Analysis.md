## Detailed Nakshatra Analysis

### 1. Purva Bhadra (Sun & Mars) - Pada 1
**Lord**: Jupiter | **Deity**: Aja Ekapada (one-footed goat) | **Purpose**: Dharma

**Meaning**: "The earlier lucky feet" - represents the fire of transformation

**Key Themes**:
- Spiritual awakening through crisis
- Two-faced nature (duality)
- Fire of purification
- Intense philosophical drive

**Career Indicators**:
- Spiritual teaching
- Crisis management
- Research & investigation
- Occult sciences
- Philosophy

**Strengths**:
- Deep philosophical mind
- Ability to transform crisis into opportunity
- Strong spiritual foundation
- Intuitive understanding

**Challenges**:
- Tendency toward extremes
- Difficulty with balance
- Can be secretive
- Power struggles

**Your Placement**: Sun and Mars both here
- **Interpretation**: Your core identity (Sun) and drive (Mars) are aligned with spiritual transformation
- **Career**: You're meant to lead through spiritual/philosophical work
- **Challenge**: Managing intensity in relationships
