## SPECIFIC WRITING PROJECTS

### Project 1: Philosophy Journal (Weekly)

**What**: Write 500-1000 words weekly on philosophical topics you ALREADY understand deeply

**Format**:
- **Monday**: Choose a philosophy topic you know well
- **Tuesday-Wednesday**: Write 500 words on it
- **Thursday**: Add personal insights and connections
- **Friday**: Edit and polish
- **Weekend**: Share or publish

**Topics to Start With**:
- What you believe about life (your personal philosophy)
- Lessons you've learned from experience
- Wisdom you want to pass on
- Observations about human nature

**Why This Works**:
- Goes DEEPER into what you know (Ketu mastery)
- Builds REAL confidence from REAL depth
- Creates a BODY of work over time
- Shares your wisdom with others
