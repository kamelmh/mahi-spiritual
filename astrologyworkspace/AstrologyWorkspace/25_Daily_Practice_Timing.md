**5:00-5:15 AM - Meditation (Clarity)**

- Focus on BREATH
- Set INTENTION for the day
- **Mantra**: "I am CLEAR in my PURPOSE"
- **Addresses**: Sun octile Neptune

**5:15-5:30 AM - Journaling (Emotions)**

- **Purpose**: Process emotions REGULARLY
- **Addresses**: Moon octile Pluto
