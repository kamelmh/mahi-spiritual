## The 3 Practices

### 1. Fajr — Yunus Dhikr (100x)
**Time:** Before sunrise (Fajr prayer)
**Duration:** ~15 minutes
**Purpose:** Protection for Moon in Pisces, emotional purification

**Invocation:**
> **"La ilaha illa Anta, Subhanaka, inni kuntu min al-dhalimeen"**
> (There is no god but You, Glory be to You, indeed I have been among the wrongdoers)
> — Surah Al-Anbiya 21:87

**Method:**
1. After Fajr prayer, sit in prayer mat
2. Say "Astaghfirullah" 3x
3. Begin Yunus dhikr with tasbih (108 beads recommended)
4. Complete 100 repetitions
5. Make dua for emotional protection and clarity

**Benefits:**
- Protection from anxiety and emotional turmoil
- Purification of Moon (debilitated in Pisces)
- Connection to Prophet Yunus's patience and gratitude

---

### 2. Dhuhr — Ar-Rahman Reflection (5 minutes)
**Time:** After Dhuhr prayer (midday)
**Duration:** ~5 minutes
**Purpose:** Activate Jupiter exalted in Cancer, align with teaching purpose

**Reflection Questions:**
1. **"What did Allah teach me today?"** — Journal one insight
2. **"How am I teaching others?"** — Reflect on teaching moments
3. **"What wisdom am I sharing?"** — Identify one teaching opportunity

**Invocation:**
> **"Ar-Rahman, allamal-quran, khalaqal-insan, allamahu'l-bayan"**
> (The Most Merciful, taught the Quran, created man, taught him eloquence)
> — Surah Ar-Rahman 55:1-4

**Method:**
1. After Dhuhr prayer, sit quietly
2. Recite Ar-Rahman 55:1-4
3. Journal one insight from the morning
4. Identify one teaching moment from today
5. Make dua for wisdom and teaching ability

**Benefits:**
- Activates Jupiter exalted in Cancer (12°47')
- Aligns with teaching purpose (Sun in Purva Bhadra)
- Connects to Quran as source of wisdom

---

### 3. Writing — Al-Qalam Invocation (Before Writing)
**Time:** Before any writing session
**Duration:** ~1 minute
**Purpose:** Bless writing, activate Mercury in Aquarius (4°39')

**Invocation:**
> **"Nun, wal-qalami wa ma yasturun"**
> (Nun. By the pen and what they inscribe)
> — Surah Al-Qalam 68:1

**Method:**
1. Before opening document or starting to write
2. Say "Bismillah" 3x
3. Recite Al-Qalam 68:1
4. Ask Allah to bless the writing
5. Begin writing

**Benefits:**
- Blesses all writing sessions
- Activates Mercury in Dhanishta (writing innovation)
- Connects writing to divine inspiration

## Tips for Success

1. **Start Small:** Begin with 33 repetitions of Yunus dhikr, increase to 100
2. **Set Alarms:** Fajr, Dhuhr, and Writing time reminders
3. **Journal:** Keep a small notebook for Ar-Rahman reflections
4. **Consistency:** Missing one day is okay, don't miss two
5. **Flexibility:** If Fajr is missed, do Yunus dhikr after sunrise
6. **Gratitude:** End each day with "Alhamdulillah" 10x
