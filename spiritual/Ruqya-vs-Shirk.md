# Ruqya vs Shirk

> **Important distinction** — Lawful ruqya vs shirk. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Tawheed-in-Ruqya]], [[Conditions-for-Valid-Ruqya]]

Ruqya must be free from shirk. Any ruqya involving calling on other than Allah, using talismans, or believing in independent power of objects is shirk.
