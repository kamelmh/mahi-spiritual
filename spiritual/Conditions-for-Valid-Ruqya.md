# Conditions for Valid Ruqya

> **Ruqya rules** — Requirements for lawful Islamic exorcism. Links to [[00-MOC-Ruqya]], [[Tawheed-in-Ruqya]]

Valid ruqya must: 1) Use Quranic verses or authentic supplications, 2) Be in Arabic or understandable language, 3) Believe healing comes from Allah alone, 4) Not involve shirk or bid'ah.
