## 🔑 Key Teachings (Ben Halima Methodology)

### 1. Three Stages of Ruqya
1. **Self-Ruqya (الرقية على النفس)** — Always start here
2. **Ruqya on Others (الرقية على غيره)** — With consent and proper conditions
3. **Captage (الكبت)** — Last resort, binding jinn with Quran

### 2. Core Principles
- **Quranic only** — No sihr, no amulets, no names of jinn
- **Arabic language** — Recite in Arabic or with translation
- **Belief in Tawhid** — Healing comes from Allah alone
- **No shirk or bid'ah** — Pure Islamic monotheism
- **Patience required** — Multiple sessions, sometimes months

### 3. Jinn Interaction Rules
- Jinn must answer "Bi-ismillah" (In the name of Allah)
- If it refuses, it is a Shaytan — increase recitation
- Never ask jinn to leave — command them in Allah's name
- Identify the type of affliction before treating

### 4. Water and Oil Method
- Recite Quran over clean water (for drinking)
- Recite over olive oil (for applying)
- Never use taweez (paper amulets)
- The sunnah method of Ruqya

### 5. The Strong Believer
- Strong iman is the best protection
- Increase salah, dhikr, and Quran recitation
- The patient must also worship — not just the practitioner
